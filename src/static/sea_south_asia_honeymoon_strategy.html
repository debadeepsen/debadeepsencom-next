Border Control Dynamics

Visa & Entry Frameworks for Indian Citizens

Regulatory statuses are valid for Indian passports departing CCU. Always verify updated government portals before booking flight segments.

Thailand

Visa-Exempt Promo

Enjoy fee-free entry for up to 60 days. Speeds up airport departure significantly. Ensure return tickets are printed.

Processing Fee: ₹0

Vietnam

Online E-Visa

Apply via government portal (takes 3 working days). Valid for up to 90 days with options for multiple entries.

Processing Fee: ~₹2,100

Bali (Indonesia)

E-VoA (Arrival)

Fill online ahead of time (e-VoA) to easily bypass airport lines. Complete customs declarations online 72 hrs prior.

Processing Fee: IDR 500k (~₹2,700)

Maldives

Free Visa On Arrival

30-day tourist visa granted free on arrival. IMUGA health & travel portal declaration mandatory 96 hrs before takeoff.

Processing Fee: ₹0 (Pre-clearance)

Malaysia

Visa Free Entry

Enjoy standard entry up to 30 days without fees. MDAC (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card) must be registered online 3 days prior.

Processing Fee: ₹0

Sri Lanka

ETA Clearance

Fast online ETA application. Promos periodically drop entry fees to encourage local tourism.

Processing Fee: Seasonal Waivers

Bhutan

Entry Permit & SDF

Requires pre-cleared travel authorization. Unique fee structure directly supports country ecological protection programs.

SDF: ₹1,200 Per Person/Night

Advisory Alert

Indian Travelers

Keep standard physical passport photos, proof of return ticket, and bank statements printed to guarantee custom entry clearance.

Passport Validity: Min 6 Months
